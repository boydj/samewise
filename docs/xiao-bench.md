Generated by `tests/renode/run.sh` (`tools/renode/bench.py`) from the `xiao_ble/nrf52840` image running on Renode's emulated nRF52840. Emulation gives instruction counts, not cycles, and models no flash wait states, caches or real peripherals: milestone 4 measures on the board.

## Known limits of the emulation

- Bluetooth does not start in Renode: the SoftDevice Controller's `sdc_enable` fails (-33) because Renode's PPI model can't take the channels it needs. The firmware reports it and decodes regardless; Bluetooth is tested on `nrf52_bsim` instead.
- Renode models neither the CryptoCell nor the QSPI flash, so the image uses software crypto and the RNG peripheral, and leaves the QSPI flash off.
